## The problem

Many pet owners cannot afford the care their pets need. The 2025 PetSmart Charities-Gallup *State of Pet Care* study reported:

| Finding | Survey result |
|---|---:|
| U.S. dog and cat owners who skipped or declined needed veterinary care | 52% |
| Affected owners citing financial considerations | 71% |
| Owners who declined care because of cost and reported no lower-cost alternative was offered | 73% |
| Owners who had ever been offered a veterinary payment plan | 23% |

PawSight focuses on helping owners find options that fit their actual veterinary estimate.

## Example: A funding gap

| Veterinary estimate | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Care estimate | $1,650 |
| Owner can pay | $900 |
| Still needed | **$750** |

**PawSight screening concept**

- **Scratchpay:** Potential financing route. The amount is within its published range; clinic acceptance, lender approval and repayment terms require verification.
- **RedRover Urgent Care:** Not a match for this example. The remaining gap exceeds the program's published $500 threshold.
- **Clinic payment plan:** An additional question for the clinic.

## What we learned

Customer discovery included 30 pet-owner surveys, four follow-up owner interviews and one veterinary staff interview.

**Key finding:** Owners want to understand which financial options actually apply to their bills.

**Product decision:** PawSight focuses on checking published requirements and preparing case-specific application steps.

**Next test:** Compare PawSight-style screening with manual searching on accuracy and time to a usable next step.

## How PawSight works

1. **Read the estimate:** Extract the bill amount and treatment details for review.
2. **Check the options:** Compare the case with published financing and assistance requirements.
3. **Prepare next steps:** Explain relevant options, required documents, official links and reasons an option may not fit.

**Result:** A shortlist of suitable options, their requirements and clear next steps.

## What's next

Pet owners can compare the screening concept with manual searches. Veterinary staff can assess whether it fits clinic workflows.
