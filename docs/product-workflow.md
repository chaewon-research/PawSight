# Product workflow

PawSight is designed to make the next financial decision easier after a pet owner receives a veterinary estimate.

## From bill to action plan

**1. Read the estimate**

The owner uploads an estimate and enters the money available now. AI-assisted document extraction identifies the proposed treatment, total cost, and other relevant details. The owner reviews the extracted information before screening.

**2. Check funding routes**

A source-linked rules layer compares the remaining gap, urgency, location, clinic participation, and other available facts with the requirements of assistance programs and financing options.

Results are organized into three useful groups:

- **Not a match:** A published exclusion applies.
- **Worth checking:** A potential route exists, but approval or other information is still required.
- **Need more information:** A critical eligibility condition cannot yet be determined.

**3. Prepare the next step**

The owner gets a short action plan covering relevant options, required documents, official provider links, and questions to ask the clinic.

## Demo Night case example

| Item | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Veterinary estimate | $1,650 |
| Owner can pay | $900 |
| Remaining funding gap | **$750** |

- **RedRover Urgent Care:** Excluded under its published rule because at least $500 would still be needed to begin or continue treatment. [Program details](https://redrover.org/relief/urgent-care-grants/).
- **Scratchpay:** The financing range includes $750. Availability, underwriting, APR, repayment terms, and clinic participation must be checked. [Financing details](https://scratchpay.com/how-it-works/).
- **Clinic payment plan:** This is a question for the clinic, not an assumed service.

The value of screening is that these options should not be presented as interchangeable. One is excluded by a published condition; another may be available subject to approval; the third depends on the clinic.

## Reliability principles

- Link eligibility claims to official sources and verification dates.
- Keep uncertain eligibility separate from confirmed exclusions.
- Do not imply that a lender or grant provider has approved funding.
- Leave medical treatment decisions to veterinary professionals, and do not suggest delaying emergency care for an application.
- Allow users to correct document-extraction errors before screening.
