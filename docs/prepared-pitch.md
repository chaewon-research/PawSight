# PawSight | Demo Night speaking script

*Prepared for the three-minute concept walkthrough at TEL Demo Night, November 19, 2026.*

Americans spend over forty billion dollars a year on veterinary care and related products.

Yet more than half of pet owners have skipped or declined needed care.

So what's going wrong?

Imagine your dog needs urgent treatment. You search for financial help and find a charity that supports veterinary emergencies. Sounds perfect.

But after going through the requirements, you discover that your situation doesn't qualify.

You found financial help. You just can't use it.

That's why I'm building PawSight.

**PawSight is an AI-powered financial navigator that turns a veterinary bill into a personalized funding action plan.**

It helps owners find out which options might work, which won't, and what they need to do next.

I surveyed thirty pet owners and conducted five interviews, including one with veterinary staff.

Among owners who had searched for financial assistance, most struggled to understand eligibility, and nearly half had abandoned an application.

That revealed something important.

**The problem isn't always finding financial resources. It's knowing whether you can actually use them.**

And that changed my original idea.

I initially considered predicting veterinary costs. But knowing what treatment costs doesn't explain how to pay for it.

Today, Scratch Pay provides financing. RedRover offers emergency grants. Help My Pet guides owners toward funding options, while FareVet analyzes veterinary bills.

These solutions address important parts of the problem.

PawSight is designed to connect those decisions around the owner's actual bill.

Here's how.

You upload the estimate and enter what you can afford. PawSight calculates the remaining cost, checks program requirements, explains why options may or may not fit, and helps prepare the next steps.

For example, it can rule out an emergency grant because the remaining funding gap exceeds the program's limit, while flagging a financing option that deserves further checking.

My next step is to test PawSight against existing services to see whether it helps owners reach valid financial options faster, with fewer mistakes.

**My vision is to make a clear financial action plan available alongside every veterinary estimate, so finding the right help doesn't become another barrier to care.**
