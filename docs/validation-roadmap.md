# Validation plan

The next milestone is to determine whether estimate-based screening improves financial navigation in practice.

## First evaluation

**Reference set:** Document the published terms for 8 to 12 assistance and financing programs, noting the date each rule was checked.

**Case set:** Build 12 to 15 realistic scenarios, including small and large funding gaps, urgent and nonurgent care, clinics that do not accept a lender, owners who do not want credit, incomplete estimates, and cases with no suitable option.

**Comparison:** Give participants the same scenarios and compare two workflows: searching existing funding resources versus using a structured PawSight-style screening result.

**Measures:**

| Measure | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Correct eligibility decisions | Whether the right exclusions and possible routes are identified |
| False-positive suggestions | Whether owners are sent toward a program that clearly cannot help |
| Time to a usable next step | Whether screening reduces effort |
| Explanation quality | Whether owners understand the result and what remains unknown |
| Application readiness | Whether owners know the required documents and next action |

Record individual case outcomes and errors, not just aggregate success percentages. Include difficult scenarios and a conventional-resource baseline.

## Product development

1. **Rules library:** Maintain source links, update dates, eligibility criteria, and exclusions.
2. **Estimate intake:** Extract amounts and treatment details with user confirmation.
3. **Screening workflow:** Separate exclusions, conditional options, and missing information.
4. **Action plan:** Present plain-language reasoning and official next steps.
5. **Clinic pilot:** Test usefulness and staff time with a small group of partner clinics.

## Commercial direction

One model worth testing is a clinic-facing subscription that helps staff and clients navigate financing and assistance. The first commercial milestones are workflow fit, measurable time savings, and willingness to pay. A clinic pilot will help determine whether that approach is viable.
