# References

Public sources used to frame the market problem, evaluate program conditions, and compare existing services.

## Market and access

- [PetSmart Charities-Gallup, 2025 pet-owner study](https://news.gallup.com/poll/659057/pet-owners-skipped-declined-veterinary-care.aspx). National survey of 2,498 U.S. dog and cat owners; source of the 52% and 73% figures.
- [Gallup, 2026 veterinarian study](https://news.gallup.com/poll/700115/veterinarians-say-cost-main-driver-declined-care.aspx). Perspective of 933 practicing U.S. companion-animal veterinarians.
- [American Pet Products Association, industry spending](https://americanpetproducts.org/industry-trends-and-stats). Source of the $41 billion 2025 U.S. veterinary-care and related-products sales figure. This is an industry spending figure, not a software market estimate.
